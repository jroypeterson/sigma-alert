# #558 / #561 — what the 2026-10-07 close `partial` and the 2026-10-06 midday `coverage-floor` actually were

Measured 2026-10-08 00:00-00:15 ET (overnight worker). Sources: GitHub Actions logs
of close run `37711190068` (2026-10-07, started 21:06 ET) and both CI midday runs on
2026-10-06; `cache/skip_log.json`; the local runner's `readable/return_map.cycles.json`,
git reflog and scheduled-task table; Slack #stock-price-alerts / #status-reports;
FMP `stable/quote` + `historical-price-eod/light` re-probed for all 78 US names
(~160 calls, paid Starter tier); Yahoo daily history re-pulled for 9 names.

(`gap_before_today ×2` in the FMP tally = TALK and WBD, 1 day of history; they are

**Root cause.** (c) vendor: Yahoo had no 2026-10-07 bar for **700 of 756** names at
21:06 ET (the CI cron is 17:30 ET; GitHub started it 3h36m late), the re-fetch
recovered 0/700. Our FMP fallback recovered 619/699, and **its own date guard
refused 75 correct closes**: every one of the 75 is NYSE-listed (Nasdaq names stamp
16:00:01 and passed). The guard accepts only a quote stamped in `[16:00:00, 16:01:00)`.
On 2026-10-07 NYSE closing-auction prints reached FMP's quote either late
(16:01-16:03) or never (quote frozen at the last continuous trade, 15:59:5x):

* the 43 late stamps: FMP quote price **==** FMP EOD row **==** Yahoo's (now
  backfilled) close, to the cent, in every case probed;
* the 32 pre-auction stamps: FMP quote price ≠ official close by 0-4 bp
  (C 127.29 vs 127.32, MLM 478.83 vs 479.01, KEYS 381.49 vs 381.42); FMP's EOD row
  **==** Yahoo's close in all 9 cross-checked names.
* FMP's quote does **not** move after hours: probed at 00:00 ET 2026-10-08 every
  stamp was still ≤ 16:02:57 (AAPL 16:00:01), which settles the premise the 1-minute
  window was built on ("whether FMP's `price` moves after hours is UNVERIFIED").

So the `partial` heartbeat was the **correct severity** (79 names genuinely went
unscored, and with the watermark kept they will never be scored for 10-07); the
defect is ours — the recovery path threw away 75 good closes. Without them coverage
would have been 745/756 = 98.5% (`ok`).

## #561 — the 2026-10-06 midday "coverage-floor"

* The run was the **local** `SigmaAlert-midday` task (started 13:13 ET, 38 min late),
  not CI (both CI middays that day were `ok`).
* `coverage-floor` is NOT the screen-coverage floor. That gate stamps `publish-gate`.
  The `coverage-floor` stamp is written only by the **return-map** gate
  (`return_map_is_publishable`, the ~43-symbol ETF universe and its separate
  800-day period-returns download).
* The digest **was posted**: #stock-price-alerts has the local midday message at
  13:18:23 EDT, the same second as the stamp. "Produced no output" is wrong; only
  `return_map.html` was left untouched (by design).
* Why the return-map universe fell short is **unrecoverable**: the local runner keeps
  no log, `git reset --hard` replaced its skip log at 16:25, and its `.env` has **no
  `SLACK_STATUS_REPORTS_WEBHOOK`**, so the heartbeat that carries the breakdown was
  printed into the scheduled task's void.
* Not the #551 stale-bar condition, not related to #558.

## Changes

### 1. FMP date guard (#558) — `fmp_close_bar` / `fmp_fallback`
* Window end `16:01:00` → `16:10:00` (measured max 16:02:57; ~3x margin).
* Window start `16:00:00` → `15:59:00`, but a stamp in `[15:59:00, 16:00:00)` is
  accepted **only when the run itself is at or after the settle time (17:00 ET in round 0; 16:30 ET after Fable R1, see below)**; before
  that it stays `intraday`. Rationale: the quote is the last continuous trade, and
  the EOD row is only trusted as the official close once the session has settled
  (the 2026-09-30 11:20 ET probe saw a partial EOD row intraday). CI close is
  scheduled 17:30 ET, so production always qualifies; a hand-run before 17:00 does not.
* **Close = the EOD row's price**, not the quote's, for every accepted name. The EOD
  row must still agree with the quote within `FMP_EOD_AGREE_TOL` (5 bp) — unchanged.
  High/Low are widened to include the close if the auction printed outside the
  continuous range.
* Refusal reasons keep their names (`intraday`, `after_close_window`) so the
  heartbeat histogram stays comparable.

### 2. Return-map gate stamp (#561) — `main()` + `return_map.record_cycle`
* Rename the stamp `coverage-floor` → `return-map-floor`, so the freshness alert no
  longer reads like the screen floor (the intake linked it to #551 on the word alone).
* `record_cycle(..., detail=...)` stores `last_detail` (cleared on any stamp without
  one); the return-map gate passes "return map: X/43 in the screen pull, Y/43 in the
  period-returns pull; digest posted|not posted". The freshness checker already
  prints the stamp file's note; adding `last_detail` to that note is a one-line
  change in `scheduled_jobs_monitor` that I will NOT make tonight (other repo) —
  the detail is on disk for whoever reads the stamp.

## Invariants (tested, mutation-checked)
1. A quote stamped at 15:59:59 is accepted only when now ≥ settle (16:30 ET after R1) and the EOD row
   agrees; at 16:30 ET it is `intraday`.
2. An accepted bar's Close equals the EOD row's price (not the quote's).
3. Stamps at 16:02:57 accepted; 16:10:00 and 19:45 refused `after_close_window`.
4. EOD disagreement > 5 bp still refuses in every window position.
5. The return-map gate stamps `return-map-floor` with a non-empty `last_detail`;
   a later `written` stamp clears it.

## Alternatives rejected
* **Judge US-listed coverage separately / exclude foreign names** (the row's
  suggestion): 0 of 79 were foreign; it would change nothing on 10-07.
* **Count FMP guard refusals as `skipped` rather than coverage loss**: they are not
  skips — those names have no score for 10-07 and never will (watermark kept).
  Reclassifying would turn a real hole into `ok`.
* **Drop the stamp window entirely, rely on EOD agreement**: an intraday run would
  then accept a partial EOD row that equals the live quote — exactly the 11:20 ET
  shape. The window + 17:00 settle rule is what keeps that out.
* **Use the quote price for late stamps** (as now): identical on 10-07 (quote==EOD),
  but the EOD row is the official close and is what Yahoo later serves; using it
  makes FMP-recovered and Yahoo-scored names the same number.
* **Lower `DEGRADED_SCREEN_COVERAGE`**: hides the symptom.
* **Add the status webhook to the local `.env`**: right, but it needs JP's secret
  value — reported, not done.
